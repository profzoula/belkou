Session 1 — UI/UX Design Styles


Lè w ap bati yon aplikasyon ak Cursor, Claude oswa lòt zouti IA, ou pa sèlman bezwen kòd ki mache — ou bezwen yon idantite vizyèl ki klè. Chak ekran ou kreye gen yon stil : senp, modèn, nwa, glass, editorial, elatriye.


Nan modil sa a, ou pral dekouvri 19 stil UI/UX ki pi popilè nan devlopman web ak mobile jodi a. Pou chak stil, gen yon template imaj ou ka montre AI a pou li konprann egzakteman sa w vle — olye de di sèlman « fè li bèl ».


Objektif Chapit la


Lè ou fin etidye chapit sa a, ou pral kapab :

  • Rekonèt chak stil UI/UX ak karakteristik prensipal li yo
  • Chwazi stil ki matche ak pwojè MVP ou a (BelKou, SaaS, e-commerce, elatriye)
  • Itilize template imaj yo kòm referans nan prompt Cursor / Claude
  • Evite melanje twòp stil sou menm paj la
  • Kominike ak AI a ak mo espesifik (spacing, kontrast, elevation, grid…)


1.1 Poukisa stil enpòtan nan VibeCoding?


Yon design style se yon seri règ vizyèl ki detèmine kijan koulè, tipografi, espas, lonbraj ak layout yo konpòte yo. San stil klè :

  • AI a devine — epi chak ekran sanble yon lòt app
  • Ou refè menm paj la plizyè fwa paske rezilta a « pa sanble sa w te imajine »
  • MVP ou a parèt « fini san fini » — fonksyonèl men pa pwofesyonèl


Bon pratik : chwazi 1 stil prensipal + 1 stil aksan (eg. Modern + Dark Mode pou night theme). Pa melanje Brutalist ak Luxury sou menm paj la.


1.2 Minimalist


Minimalist — senp, anpil whitespace, eleman sèlman ki nesesè. Pa gen dekò initil.



  • Karakteristik : anpil espas vid, ti palette koulè, tit klè, ti kantite bouton
  • Lè pou itilize : landing page, dashboard pwòp, app ki bezwen fokis sou kontni
  • Mo kle pou AI : « minimal, generous whitespace, single accent color, no decoration »


1.3 Modern


Modern — clean, contemporary, bon typography ak spacing. Se stil ki pi souvan itilize nan SaaS 2024–2026.



  • Karakteristik : sans-serif, radius modere, gradients diskrè, layout aè
  • Lè pou itilize : BelKou, fòmasyon, startup tech, dashboard
  • Mo kle pou AI : « modern SaaS UI, rounded-2xl, subtle gradient hero, clean hierarchy »


1.4 Clean UI


Clean UI — interface ki trè òganize ak fasil pou itilize. Chak seksyon gen wòl klè.



  • Karakteristik : grid regilye, separasyon klè, navbar + sidebar + kontni
  • Lè pou itilize : admin panel, settings, fòm konplèks
  • Mo kle pou AI : « clean organized layout, clear sections, consistent padding, readable labels »


1.5 Neumorphism


Neumorphism — soft shadows, eleman ki sanble soti nan background lan (soft UI / soft emboss).



  • Karakteristik : background ak eleman menm ton, doub lonbraj (klere + fènwa)
  • Lè pou itilize : kontwòl, bouton, widget — pa pou tout paj
  • Atansyon : kontras aksè souvan ba — verifye WCAG
  • Mo kle pou AI : « neumorphic cards, soft inset/outset shadows, monochromatic surface »


1.6 Glassmorphism


Glassmorphism — transparans, blur, glass cards sou background ki gen koulè.



  • Karakteristik : backdrop-blur, opacity, border klè, gradient background
  • Lè pou itilize : hero, modal, navbar sou foto / gradient
  • Mo kle pou AI : « glassmorphism, frosted glass card, bg-white/20 backdrop-blur-xl, subtle border »


1.7 Bento Grid


Bento Grid — kontni òganize nan diferan cards/blocks (inspire pa layout Apple / Notion).



  • Karakteristik : blòk asimetrik, gwosè varye, grid responsif
  • Lè pou itilize : homepage, dashboard analytics, showcase features
  • Mo kle pou AI : « bento grid layout, mixed card sizes, dark tiles, feature highlights »


1.8 Dark Mode


Dark Mode — background nwa/dark ak accents lumineux. Pa sèlman « mete nwa » — li mande kontras ak hierarchy.



  • Karakteristik : zinc/slate palette, teks klè, accent violet/cyan/blue
  • Lè pou itilize : dev tools, player videyo, app itilize sou lannwit
  • Mo kle pou AI : « dark mode UI, zinc-900 background, muted foreground, purple accent »


1.9 Brutalist


Brutalist — gwo typography, kontrast fò, design ki pa konvansyonèl. Bold ak raw.



  • Karakteristik : border epè, koulè primè, monospace, pa gen radius
  • Lè pou itilize : portfolio kreatif, landing eksperimantal — pa pou bank oswa legal
  • Mo kle pou AI : « brutalist web design, thick black borders, bold type, high contrast blocks »


1.10 Swiss Style


Swiss / Swiss Style — grid presi, typography fò, layout trè estriktire (International Typographic Style).



  • Karakteristik : Helvetica-like, grid vizib, asymetri kontwole, ti palette
  • Lè pou itilize : corporate, edikasyon, dokiman ki bezwen kredibilite
  • Mo kle pou AI : « swiss design grid, strong typographic hierarchy, red accent line, structured layout »


1.11 Editorial


Editorial — style magazin, gwo tit, bon itilizasyon imaj ak typography (serif + layout narratif).



  • Karakteristik : serif headings, kolòn teks, imaj gwo, rytm lekti
  • Lè pou itilize : blog, leson atik, about page, storytelling
  • Mo kle pou AI : « editorial magazine layout, large serif headline, article columns, hero image »


1.12 Material Design


Material Design — cards, elevation, components ak règ byen defini (Google).



  • Karakteristik : elevation (lonbraj), FAB, ripple, primary color solid
  • Lè pou itilize : Android-like apps, fòm, dashboards enterprise
  • Mo kle pou AI : « material design 3, elevated cards, primary app bar, rounded buttons »


1.13 Flat Design


Flat Design — pa gen anpil shadows oswa 3D ; simple shapes ak colors.



  • Karakteristik : koulè solid, pa gen gradient, ikòn senp
  • Lè pou itilize : infographic, onboarding rapid, UI ki bezwen vitès chaj
  • Mo kle pou AI : « flat design, solid color blocks, no shadows, simple icons »


1.14 3D UI


3D UI — eleman 3D, depth ak visual effects (illustration, mockup, hero).



  • Karakteristik : perspective, isometric, lighting, objè floating
  • Lè pou itilize : landing tech, gaming, produit SaaS premium
  • Mo kle pou AI : « 3D UI hero, isometric elements, depth and lighting, floating cards »


1.15 Futuristic


Futuristic — teknoloji, gradients, neon/accent effects, sans serif sharp.



  • Karakteristik : ligne glow, HUD-like, cyan/purple, dark bg
  • Lè pou itilize : AI products, crypto, innovation labs
  • Mo kle pou AI : « futuristic tech UI, neon accents, hud lines, dark sci-fi dashboard »


1.16 Aurora / Gradient UI


Aurora / Gradient UI — gradients ak background ki gen koulè fluid (mesh, aurora blobs).



  • Karakteristik : radial gradients, blur blobs, glass cards sou top
  • Lè pou itilize : hero BelKou-style, signup, pricing
  • Mo kle pou AI : « aurora gradient background, mesh colors, soft glow blobs, glass overlay »


1.17 Claymorphism


Claymorphism — soft 3D shapes, rounded corners ak shadows (bouk argile dijital).



  • Karakteristik : pastel, gwo radius, doub shadow, volum ki mou
  • Lè pou itilize : apps friendly, kids, onboarding jwe
  • Mo kle pou AI : « claymorphism UI, soft 3D buttons, pastel blue, chunky rounded shapes »


1.18 Retro / Vintage


Retro / Vintage — enspirasyon nan ansyen interfaces ak typography (80s, 90s, terminal).



  • Karakteristik : sepia/cream, monospace, pixel ou CRT vibe
  • Lè pou itilize : branding nostaljik, evènman, side projects kreatif
  • Mo kle pou AI : « retro vintage UI, warm paper tones, monospace terminal block, 80s aesthetic »


1.19 Cyberpunk


Cyberpunk — dark UI, neon colors (pink/cyan), futuristic aesthetic urban.



  • Karakteristik : nwa pwofon, neon magenta/cyan, grid scanlines
  • Lè pou itilize : gaming, music, entertainment — pa pou healthcare
  • Mo kle pou AI : « cyberpunk UI, neon pink and cyan, dark grid, glitch accents »


1.20 Luxury / Premium


Luxury / Premium — elegant typography, anpil whitespace, subtle details (lò, nwa, serif).



  • Karakteristik : serif, gold/black, ti border, pa gen koulè klè
  • Lè pou itilize : coaching premium, fashion, high-ticket services
  • Mo kle pou AI : « luxury premium UI, gold accents on black, serif typography, generous spacing »

Apre ou fini li


  1. Chwazi 2 stil ki matche ak MVP ou a
  2. Ouvri template SVG yo nan navigatè pou wè referans yo
  3. Ekri 1 prompt nan Cursor ak RTFC + non stil la
  4. Verifye kontras ak lekti sou mobil (DevTools responsive)
  5. Poze kesyon nan forum kou a si yon stil pa klè


Konsèy final : yon bon UI se pa plis efè — se konvansyon ki repete. Chwazi yon stil, aplike l sou tout paj yo, epi itilize AI pou polish — pa pou chanje direksyon chak fwa.
